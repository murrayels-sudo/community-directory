Share this app

Anyone can scan this code to open Community Directory and add it to their own phone — free, and works offline once installed.

murrayels-sudo.github.io/community-directory

Privacy

No accounts, no sign-in

You don’t give your name or any details to use this app. What you search for or tap is not sent anywhere or recorded.

What is saved on your phone

A copy of the directory, so it works without internet, and a few small settings, such as whether you closed the install reminder. This stays on your phone.

Nearby areas

This uses suburb names only. The app does not ask for your phone’s location.

Counting installs

The first time the app is opened from your home screen, it sends one anonymous count to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused counting service. This lets us report how many people have installed it. It does not include your name, searches or a device ID. See goatcounter.com/privacy.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub. Like any website host, GitHub can see technical connection details when the app downloads or checks for updates.

Calls, websites and email

Tapping a phone number, website or email address opens your phone’s own calling, web or email app. That organisation then handles your contact.

If someone else might see your phone

The app uses a plain name and icon. To remove it, press and hold the icon and choose to remove or delete it. In an emergency, call 000.
